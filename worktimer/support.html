Getting started

  • Check in / out: Tap the big button to start tracking, and again to stop. The timer runs live while you're checked in.
  • Set your goal: In Settings, choose your weekly hours and work days. WorkTimer shows your progress toward today's and this week's target.
  • Breaks: If you check back in shortly after checking out, WorkTimer continues your previous session instead of starting a new one. You can change the threshold in Settings.
  • Fix a mistake: Use the + menu to add a past session, or tap any entry in History to edit it.

WorkTimer Pro

WorkTimer is free. A one-time purchase (no subscription) unlocks the Apple Watch app, smart adjusting targets, time off, banked overtime, target notifications, Siri Shortcuts & WiFi automations, CSV export, and unlimited history.

I already paid for WorkTimer — do I have to buy Pro again?

No. If you downloaded WorkTimer while it was a paid app, Pro is unlocked for you automatically, at no cost. If it doesn't appear, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

I bought Pro but don't see it on another device.

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings.

Why is older history missing on the free plan?

The free plan keeps your most recent two months of sessions. Unlock Pro to keep your complete history or choose how long to keep sessions.

Apple Watch & automation (Pro)

  • Apple Watch: Check in and out from your wrist; sessions sync automatically with your iPhone.
  • Siri: Say things like "Check in to work" once you've added the shortcut.
  • WiFi automations: Use the Shortcuts app to check in automatically when you join your work network. See the in-app guide under Settings → Automation.

Your data

  • Sync: With iCloud enabled, your data syncs privately across your devices through your own iCloud account.
  • Export: Pro users can export any date range to CSV for invoicing or analysis.
  • Privacy: WorkTimer collects nothing and has no trackers. See the Privacy Policy.
