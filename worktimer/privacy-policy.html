Privacy Policy

WorkTimer · Last updated 30 June 2026

WorkTimer is built to be private by design. The short version: the app collects nothing about you, and your data never reaches us.

WorkTimer does not collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers or SDKs of any kind.

Your data stays yours

  • Your work sessions and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • If you enable iCloud, your data syncs across your own devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), inside your private iCloud account. It is not stored on, or accessible to, the developer's servers — we have none.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. It has no user accounts and no login.

Notifications

If you turn on target-reached notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to us or to any third party.

Purchases

WorkTimer is free to download. An optional one-time purchase, WorkTimer Pro, unlocks additional features. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; payment is processed by Apple and we never receive your payment details. Your purchase status is verified through Apple's StoreKit, and customers who bought WorkTimer when it was a paid app keep Pro automatically. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

WorkTimer is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date above.
