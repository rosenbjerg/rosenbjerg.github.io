Privacy Policy

KeepingTabs · Last updated: 16 June 2026

KeepingTabs is built to be private by design. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your personal data.
  • Your tasks stay on your device and sync only through your own private iCloud account.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.
  • We never sell or share your data, because we never receive it in the first place.

Information you create

Everything you enter in KeepingTabs — task names, schedules, completion history, list names, and the local member names you add to a shared list — is stored on your device using Apple's on-device database. We, the developer, have no server that receives or stores this content.

iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, KeepingTabs uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your own Apple devices. This data is held in your private iCloud account, governed by your relationship with Apple and Apple's privacy practices. We cannot read the contents of your private iCloud database. If you are not signed into iCloud, your data simply remains local to the device.

Shared lists

KeepingTabs lets you share a list with other people through Apple's iCloud sharing. When you share a list, the tasks and activity on that list become visible to the people you invite, through iCloud. You choose what to share and with whom, and you can stop sharing at any time. This sharing happens between you and the people you invite via Apple's infrastructure; the developer is not a party to it and does not receive the shared content.

Notifications

KeepingTabs schedules local notifications on your device to remind you when tasks are due. These are generated and delivered entirely on your device; no notification content is sent to us or any third party.

Purchases

KeepingTabs offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the ability to share lists. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card, or other payment details — only the anonymous confirmation from Apple needed to unlock the feature on your devices. Restoring purchases relies on your Apple ID, managed by Apple.

Analytics and tracking

KeepingTabs contains no analytics frameworks, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind. The app does not include the iOS App Tracking Transparency tracking that would require your permission, because it performs no such tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own beyond Apple's iCloud and App Store services described above.

Children

KeepingTabs does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages and contains no data collection.

Your control over your data

You can delete any task, list, or your entire data set from within the app at any time. To remove data synced via iCloud, you can also manage KeepingTabs' iCloud storage in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and post the revised version here.
