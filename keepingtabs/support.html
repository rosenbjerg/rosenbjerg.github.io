Frequently asked questions

What does "self-correcting schedule" actually mean?

You tell KeepingTabs how often a task should happen — for example, three times every two weeks. When you complete it early, the next due date eases out a little; when you complete it late, KeepingTabs nudges upcoming dates closer so you gradually catch back up. The correction is spread over several completions, so a single off day never whiplashes your whole schedule.

How do I set how often a task repeats?

Each task has a cadence expressed as a number of completions over a number of days — "1 per 7 days," "3 per 14 days," and so on. You can also set preferred weekdays to gently steer a task toward certain days without locking it to a fixed date.

Can I have a task stop on its own?

Yes. Besides running indefinitely, a task can end on a chosen date or after a set number of completions. Once it's done it moves into a Completed section so it's out of your way, and you can reopen it any time to start it up again.

What if I want to skip just one cycle?

Skip the current cycle and KeepingTabs moves on to the next one without counting it against you or breaking your rhythm. Skips are recorded in the task's history, so if you skip by mistake you can undo it and pick up where you left off.

I forgot to mark something done. Can I add it later?

Yes. You can log a completion on the date it actually happened, and edit or remove a past completion from the task's history. Because your history is what drives the schedule, the due dates recalculate to match as soon as you do.

Can I complete or snooze a task from the notification?

Yes. Reminders include Complete and Snooze 1 day actions, so you can act without opening the app. To receive reminders, allow notifications when prompted (or enable them later in iOS Settings → Notifications → KeepingTabs).

Can I use Siri or Shortcuts?

Yes. You can complete, snooze, or skip a task by voice with Siri, and build your own automations with the Shortcuts app — all without opening KeepingTabs.

When do reminders arrive, and can I change the time?

By default reminders arrive at 09:00 on the day a task is due. You can change the default reminder time in the app's settings, and individual tasks can override it with their own time.

I'm going away. Can I pause a task?

Yes. Pause a task to hide it from your list and stop its reminders. You can pause it until a specific date — it then resumes automatically when that date arrives, picking up from the present rather than resurfacing as long overdue.

How does sharing with family or roommates work?

You can share a list with other people through iCloud. Everyone on the list sees the same tasks, and you can assign a task to a person or let it rotate automatically through the group. An activity feed shows who completed what. Sharing a list is unlocked with a single one-time purchase; joining a list that someone else shares with you is always free.

I bought the sharing unlock but it isn't showing on my other device.

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. On the other device, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then use the "Restore Purchases" option on the sharing screen. The unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so it never expires.

Does my data sync across my devices?

Yes. When you're signed into iCloud, your tasks sync automatically across your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud account. If you're not signed into iCloud, KeepingTabs keeps everything locally on the device.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

Your data is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account. We don't run a server and we can't see your tasks. The only data shared with anyone else is what you explicitly choose to share via a shared list. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is there a widget?

Yes — KeepingTabs offers Home Screen widgets (small, medium, large) and Lock Screen widgets that show your due count and upcoming tasks at a glance. From the Home Screen widget you can tap a task to complete it on the spot, and a Control Center button lets you finish your next due task in a single tap.
