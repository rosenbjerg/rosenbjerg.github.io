Frequently asked questions

How does the smart watering work?

Log each watering with a tap. After two or more records, Water of Life works out each plant's natural interval and adjusts it for the season — longer gaps in winter, shorter in summer — based on your latitude and hemisphere. You can always set a fixed interval per plant instead.

Why isn't a plant showing as "needs watering" yet?

A new plant needs a couple of logged waterings before it can learn a rhythm. Until then it uses a sensible fallback interval, or the fixed interval you set. You can also set how long ago it was last watered when you add it.

What's included free, and what does Pro add?

Water of Life is free for up to 10 plants, including smart seasonal learning, watering reminders, photos, the repotting log, and search. A single one-time Pro purchase (no subscription) adds unlimited plants, full fertilizer tracking and reminders, custom groups, and the "Water All" / "Fertilize All" batch actions.

I bought the app before it was free. Do I have to pay again?

No. If you purchased Water of Life while it was a paid download, Pro is unlocked for you automatically — there's nothing to buy or restore.

I already had Pro but a new device shows it locked.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Your plants and history sync automatically over iCloud once you're signed in.

Will my plants sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Water of Life syncs privately through your own iCloud account. Make sure iCloud is enabled and you're signed in with the same Apple Account on each device.

I'm not getting reminders.

Reminders are opt-in. Enable them in the app's Settings, then check that notifications are allowed for Water of Life in the iOS Settings app. You can pick the time of day reminders arrive; fertilizer reminders (Pro) have their own toggle.
