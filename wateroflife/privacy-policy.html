Privacy Policy

Water of Life · Last updated 3 July 2026

Water of Life is built to be private by design. We don't collect your data, and there's nothing to collect it with — no account, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.

What we collect

Nothing. Water of Life has no user accounts and no sign-up. We, the developer, have no servers that receive your information and we never see your plants, photos, notes, or care history.

Where your data lives

The plants, groups, photos, notes, and watering and fertilizer history you create are stored on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, this data also syncs through your own private iCloud account so it stays in step across your iPhone and iPad.

iCloud sync is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud service and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Your data is stored in your personal iCloud database, which the developer cannot access. You can turn iCloud sync off for the app in the iOS Settings app at any time.

Photos and camera

If you add a photo to a plant, Water of Life uses your camera or photo library only for that purpose, and only when you choose to. Photos you add are stored with your plant data on your device (and in your private iCloud, if enabled). They are not uploaded anywhere else.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device. If you enable them, iOS delivers them; no reminder content leaves your device.

Purchases

Water of Life offers a single one-time "Pro" purchase through Apple's App Store. Payments and purchase verification are handled by Apple — the app never sees your payment details. Purchase handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are none. Water of Life contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Water of Life does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
