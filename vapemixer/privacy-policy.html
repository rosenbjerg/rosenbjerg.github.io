VapeMixer Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 15, 2025

Overview

VapeMixer is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter stays on your device.

Data Collection

VapeMixer does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No usage or analytics data is collected.
  • No data is sent to any server or third party.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used.
  • No account or login is required.

Data Storage

All data you create in VapeMixer — including recipes, ingredients, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

If you delete the app, all associated data is removed from your device.

Third-Party Services

VapeMixer does not integrate with or use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

VapeMixer is not directed at children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
